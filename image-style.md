# Wolfbud — Image Style Guide

Visual direction for Wolfbud brand imagery, primarily the **macOS app icon**.

## Brand in one line

Wolfbud is a friendly, always-there voice companion for your Mac — speech-to-text
and text-to-speech, one hotkey away. The mark should feel approachable ("bud" =
buddy) but sharp and modern (a wolf), and hint at voice/audio.

## Core concept: Wolf + soundwave

A single, bold **wolf head** as the focal mark, integrated with a **soundwave /
audio-equalizer motif** that signals speech and dictation. The soundwave can form
the muzzle/chin area, sit below the head like a voice cue, or radiate outward — one
clean idea, not two competing ones. Front-facing, symmetrical, geometric.

## Format

- **App-icon tile:** rounded-square (macOS "squircle") with generous padding so the
  mark reads at small sizes (menu-bar, dock).
- Single centered focal subject. No text, no wordmark, no lettering anywhere.
- Must remain legible and recognizable when scaled down to 32×32 px.

## Style

- Modern **flat / minimal vector** look. Clean geometric shapes, confident line
  weights, subtle depth allowed but no heavy skeuomorphism.
- High contrast between the wolf mark and the background tile.
- Smooth, slightly rounded corners on shapes; friendly, not aggressive or snarling.

## Color

- **Tile background:** deep indigo → violet vertical gradient (roughly `#4F46E5` to
  `#7C3AED`).
- **Wolf mark:** soft off-white / pale silver (`#F5F5FA`) so it pops off the tile.
- **Soundwave accent:** bright cyan (`#22D3EE`), used as the one energetic accent —
  the "voice" of the icon.
- Keep the palette tight: indigo/violet tile, pale wolf, cyan wave. No extra colors.

## Mood

Confident, clean, friendly, tech-forward. A premium macOS utility icon — think the
polish of a well-designed menu-bar app, not a mascot cartoon.

## Avoid

- No photorealism, no fur texture noise, no gradients-gone-muddy.
- No text, letters, or numbers.
- No busy backgrounds, no drop-shadow clutter, no realistic mic hardware.
- Nothing that looks like the ElevenLabs logo or brand — this is an independent app.
